===============================================================
  CHARTICKS - EXECUTION TERMINAL  (version 0.1.0)
===============================================================

TO INSTALL
  Double-click:  Charticks Setup 0.1.0.exe

  Windows will warn that it does not recognise the app. That is
  expected - the build is not code-signed yet.
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  There is no wizard and no setup script. It installs and opens
  by itself. You do NOT need Python or anything else.


BEFORE YOU TRADE - READ THIS
  Open KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.html.

  START IN PAPER MODE. Everything works there and no order ever
  leaves your PC. Only switch to Live once you have seen the app
  behave the way you expect.

  In LIVE mode the app DOES act on your positions by itself:
  Stop Loss, Target, Trailing Stop Loss and Portfolio Trail Profit
  all send real exit orders when they are hit.

  >> THE MOST IMPORTANT THING TO UNDERSTAND <<

  Those stops live INSIDE THIS APPLICATION. There is no stop order
  resting at the exchange.

  So if Charticks closes, your PC crashes, or your internet drops,
  NOTHING is protecting the position any more - and your broker is
  still holding it. The app warns you if you try to quit while it
  is managing live positions.

  Keep your broker's own app or website open as a backup exit. If
  you also place a stop there, be aware you could end up exiting
  twice - check your broker positions after any automatic exit.

  This has been built and tested, but NOT yet proven on a real
  account through a full trading day. Treat your first sessions as
  a test: one lot, and watch what it does.

BEFORE YOU INSTALL
  If you already have an older Charticks, UNINSTALL IT FIRST
  (Settings > Apps > Charticks > Uninstall). Earlier versions
  packaged the trading engine differently, and leftover files can
  stop the new one starting.

  You need a few GB of free disk space. The app downloads the
  day's instrument list (about 40 MB) and writes logs; on a full
  disk it re-downloads it every launch and startup gets very slow.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Send three things:
    1. A screenshot of the whole window
    2. What you did just before it happened
    3. Everything in this folder:  %APPDATA%\Charticks\logs

  The most useful files are:
    startup.log         why the app was slow to start
    sidecar-process.log why the trading engine did not start
    orders.log          what was sent to your broker, and when
    application.log     everything else

  Never share your broker credentials. Crop or blur them out of
  any screenshot first.
