===============================================================
  CHARTICKS - EXECUTION TERMINAL  (version 0.1.0)
===============================================================

TO INSTALL
  Double-click:  Charticks Setup 0.1.0.exe

  Windows will warn that it does not recognise the app. That is
  expected - the build is not code-signed yet.
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  There is no wizard and no setup script. It installs and opens
  by itself. You do NOT need Python or anything else.


BEFORE YOU TRADE - READ THIS
  Open KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.html.

  In LIVE mode you CAN open a position and close it from the app
  (fully, partially, or all at once with Square Off All).

  But NOTHING EXITS AUTOMATICALLY. Stop Loss, Target and Trailing
  Stop Loss do NOT work on live positions. A live position closes
  only when you close it.

  Keep your broker's own app or website open as a backup exit, in
  case this app, your PC or your internet fails.

  PAPER mode has no such limits - everything works there.


THE OTHER FILES
  INSTALL.html            full install guide
  BROKER-SETUP.html       connecting your broker account
  KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.html  what does not work yet  <-- read this
  TROUBLESHOOTING.html    when something goes wrong
  SHA256SUMS.txt          checksum to verify the installer

  Double-click any .html file to open it in your browser.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Send three things:
    1. A screenshot of the whole window
    2. What you did just before it happened
    3. Everything in this folder:  %APPDATA%\Charticks\logs
       (application.log is the most useful single file)

  Never share your broker credentials. Crop or blur them out of
  any screenshot first.
