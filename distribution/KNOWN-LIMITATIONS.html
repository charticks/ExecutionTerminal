Known Limitations

Charticks 0.1.0 · What does not work yet, and what that means for you

⚠ Live positions have no automatic protection

In Live mode this build sends real orders to your broker. You can open a position and close it (fully, partially, or all at once).

But Stop Loss, Target and Trailing Stop Loss do NOT work on live positions. Nothing exits automatically. A live position is only closed when you close it.

Keep your broker's own app or website open as a backup exit — if this app, your PC or your internet fails, that is your only way out.

Paper mode has none of these limits.

The specific risks

1. Nothing exits a live position automatically

You can type a Stop Loss or Target against a live position, but nothing enforces it. The risk engine only watches paper positions. A live position has no automatic protection at all — it will not exit on a stop, a target, a trailing stop, or a portfolio trail.

Set your stops in your broker's terminal, or watch the position yourself.

2. Roll shows something that did not happen

If you use Roll on a live position, the app will display a rolled position that does not exist at your broker. Nothing is sent. The original position is untouched and reappears within a few seconds when the app next reads your broker's book.

Do not use Roll in Live mode. It works correctly in Paper.

3. A resting live order cannot be changed or cancelled

A live LIMIT order that has not filled yet sits at your broker, and the app offers no way to re-price or cancel it. Use your broker's terminal for that. Working live orders are also not shown in the Positions grid — a position appears only once the order actually fills.

4. Position and trade limits do not apply in Live

Max Position, max trades and max loss are checked against the paper book, so they do not restrain live orders. Size your live trades manually.

5. A live position takes a few seconds to appear

Live positions are read from your broker roughly every 4 seconds, so a filled order shows up shortly after — not instantly like Paper. The green “Order accepted” confirmation appears immediately with the broker's order id; the position row follows.

Other things to know

Angel requires your IP to be whitelisted

Every order fails with AG7002 — “<your ip> is not a registered IP” unless your machine's public IP is registered on your own SmartAPI app. On mobile tethering or a CGNAT connection your IP changes without warning, which breaks order placement mid-session. See BROKER-SETUP.html.

There is no exchange holiday calendar

On a trading holiday the app still believes the market is open between 09:15 and 15:30 IST and will let you submit an order. Your broker rejects it. The app is not wrong about the clock, it simply does not know the holiday list.

Nothing is saved when the app restarts

Paper positions, order history and any Stop Loss / Target you configured are held in memory only. Closing the app or restarting the engine clears them. Real broker positions are unaffected — they live at the broker and reappear when the app reconnects.

Closing an ICICI session key expires daily

ICICI issues a session key by browser login that dies each day. Reconnect that account every morning before trading.

The app is not code-signed

Windows will warn on install. See INSTALL.html.

Use Paper mode for anything you are still evaluating. It runs on the same live market feed and the same execution rules as live trading — market orders fill at the ask, sells at the bid, limit orders rest until the market reaches them. The only difference is that no order leaves your machine.
